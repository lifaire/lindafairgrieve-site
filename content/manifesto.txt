## What I believe in

For marketing to become a force for change, two conditions need to be true: the goal of the project is rooted in a human-centred, sustainable solution, and the marketing practice itself is sustainable and ethical. Using good methods for a bad goal is pointless. Using bad methods for a good goal taints the goal and destroys it from within.

I believe in flipping the system: a world where each of us can find our place and meaning by contributing in our own way to a system that, at its core, supports equality and sustainability and includes everybody. Naive? Good!

## What I work towards

I want to use my skills, time and creativity to promote future-friendly initiatives. When a product or service is designed and promoted to help only a small group thrive at the expense of others, using manipulation, deceit, technology or design to chase short-term, revenue-first goals, it inevitably takes away from the wellbeing of everyone else.

I want technology to serve meaningful projects, and money to go back to being a means of exchange.

I want to build trust in marketing as a source of good. So with every brief I ask: what agenda am I helping to promote? Are the methods manipulative, or transparent and trust-restoring?

## My guiding values

- Integrity over spin | Marketing with integrity means doing the right thing over the revenue-driven thing, and asking whether the choice made today will have a long-term social impact.
- Collaboration over competition | Working towards shared, larger goals instead of a competitive mindset. In the end, we are all part of one system, with our species and the planet as the physical limits of our shared resources.
- Simplicity and clarity
- Meaningful action
- Systemic change over one-time shots
- Common good over individual gain
- Win-win-win imperative | You win as a business, your client wins, and society at large wins too.
- Slow power over short-term results
- People first, revenue second

## My most urgent priorities

- Change the narrative around marketing practices
- Prove that ethical ways deliver results
- Give sustainable projects equal access to the best, most advanced marketing tools and practices
